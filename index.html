A conversation, not a command line

Say what you want in plain words. Tayli keeps up, does the work and checks in.

Talks back instantly

She hears you and answers aloud in a natural voice, so it feels like a call, not a chat box.

Works while you talk

Ask for a job like "fix the failing test in my project". It runs in your repo in the background while you keep the conversation going.

Nothing risky without your OK

Anything with outside effects waits for you. Say yes, tap Approve, or confirm high-risk steps with Touch ID.

Your voice stays on your Mac

Tayli is local first. Here is exactly what leaves your Mac and what doesn't.

  • Speech to text runs on your Mac. Your audio is turned into text by a speech model on your own machine, and her voice is made there too.
  • Recording is off by default. If you turn it on, she tells you at the start of each call.
  • No accounts, no Tayli servers. There is nothing to sign up for, and Tayli runs no servers of its own.
  • What goes to Anthropic. To think and act, Tayli sends the text of your requests to Anthropic's Claude, through your own Claude Code sign-in. Your audio is never sent.
