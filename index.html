Tayli, a voice for the Claude Code you already use.

          

            Tayli is a voice assistant for your Mac. You talk to her like a phone call. She hears you with a speech model on your Mac, thinks with your
            own Claude Code sign-in, and answers aloud with a voice model on your Mac.
          

          
Coming soon

          
Free and open source.

        

      

      

        

          
What she does

          

            

              
Talk from your Mac or your phone

              
Hold a key, or leave her listening. From the car, join your Discord voice channel and the same Tayli answers.

            

            

              
Background tasks, with approvals

              

                Ask for a job like "fix the failing test in my project" and keep talking while it runs. Anything with outside effects asks first: say yes, tap
                Approve, or press the button she sends to your Discord DMs.
              

            

            

              
Barge in

              
Talk over her and she stops mid-sentence to listen.

            

            

              
Name only listening

              
In Name only mode she acts when you say her name. Speech she can tell isn't yours stays on your Mac; she can still mistake someone else's words for yours.

            

            

              
Reminders, timers, time and weather

              
Weather comes from Open-Meteo, free and with no key.

            

            

              
Tools she builds

              
Ask for a tool she lacks and a task writes it. A safety reviewer reads it, you approve it once, and it shows up under Settings.

            

          

        

        

          
Where your data goes

          

            Speech to text, the voice and voice ID run on your Mac. We have no server, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, so we never see your data.
            What leaves your Mac goes to Anthropic through your own Claude Code account, the pages she reads for you, Discord if you use it, a weather service and a
            stock-quote service when you ask for those, and the host her speech models download from. The privacy policy lists every flow.
          

        

        

          
Her limits

          

            Tayli makes mistakes. She can mishear you, get facts wrong, or act on the wrong thing. She is supervised help, and you stay responsible for what she
            does for you. Call recording is off by default; if you turn it on, she says so at the start of each call. You accept the terms and
            privacy policy on her first-run screen.
          

        

        

          
What you need

          
A Mac with Apple Silicon, M1 or later. macOS 13 or later. Claude Code, signed in. About 8 GB of free disk for the speech models and the build.
