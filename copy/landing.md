# Claude Code, out loud.

Tayli is a voice assistant for your Mac. You talk to her like a phone call. She hears you with a speech model on your Mac, thinks with your own Claude Code sign-in, and answers aloud with a voice model on your Mac.

Coming soon. Free and open source.

## What she does

Talk from your Mac or your phone. Hold a key, or leave her listening. From the car, join your Discord voice channel and the same Tayli answers.

Background tasks, with approvals. Ask for a job like "fix the failing test in my project" and keep talking while it runs. Anything with outside effects asks first: say yes, tap Approve, or press the button she sends to your Discord DMs.

Barge in. Talk over her and she stops mid-sentence to listen.

Name only listening. She acts when you say her name. Other speech, a TV included, stays on your Mac.

Reminders, timers, time and weather. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, free and with no key.

Tools she builds. Ask for a tool she lacks and a task writes it. A safety reviewer reads it, you approve it once, and it shows up under Settings.

## Where your data goes

Speech to text, the voice and voice ID run on your Mac. We have no server, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry, so we never see your data. What leaves your Mac goes to services you set up yourself: Anthropic through your own Claude Code account, the sites she opens for you, and Discord if you use it. The privacy policy lists every flow.

## Her limits

Tayli makes mistakes. She can mishear you, get facts wrong, or act on the wrong thing. She is supervised help, and you stay responsible for what she does for you. Calls are recorded on your Mac by default, and you can turn that off in Settings.

## What you need

A Mac with Apple Silicon, M1 or later. macOS 13 or later. Claude Code, signed in. About 8 GB of free disk for the speech models and the build.
