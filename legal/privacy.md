# Tayli Privacy Policy

**Draft: not legal advice, and not yet reviewed by counsel.**
Version 0.6 draft, 2026-11-15. Every statement below describes what this version does; anything not built yet is marked **Not in this version**, so nothing here promises more than the code does. Published by the Tayli project.

## 1. The short version
- Tayli runs on your Mac. **The Tayli project receives none of your data.** We have no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Downloading from GitHub or visiting our website is covered by their policies.
- What leaves your Mac goes only to services **you** set up: Anthropic (through your own Claude Code account), the web pages Tayli reads, Discord if you use it, and anything a background task you start contacts. Three services are contacted without an account of yours, each listed in section 3: a weather service and a stock-quote service when you ask for those, and a model host that Tayli downloads its speech models from.
- You can see, edit and delete what Tayli stores. Deleting the Tayli data folder deletes it, except copies in your own backups (section 2).

## 2. What is stored on your Mac
Tayli keeps data in three places: `~/.claude/assistant/` (settings, memories, logs, recordings), `~/Library/Application Support/Tayli/` (task work), and Claude Code's own session folder under `~/.claude/`, where Claude Code keeps transcripts of every Tayli session under its own retention settings. **Only your voiceprint is encrypted by Tayli** (AES-GCM, key in your macOS Keychain). Everything else is plain files readable by your macOS user only (permission 0600); turn on FileVault to encrypt them at rest. Time Machine, iCloud or other backups you run may copy this folder, including voiceprints; deleting in Tayli does not delete those copies.

## 4. Microphone and transcription
While Tayli is visibly running, its microphone is open all the time (always listening) or only while you hold the key (hold to listen, the default), per your setting, and speech is transcribed on your Mac, including people nearby who are not talking to Tayli. Settings > Listen to chooses what she acts on: "Everything" (the default) treats any line she hears as a possible request, and "Wake word" acts only on lines that use her name, for the current call only (when the call ends Tayli switches back to "Everything"); hold to listen on the Mac always works as "Everything" while you hold the key.

**Whose words Tayli acts on.** Tayli treats speech it hears as yours unless your voiceprint says it is someone else's. To check, it turns each spoken line into a numeric voice representation in memory, compares it with your voiceprint, and discards it; nobody else's voice model is stored. If you have no voiceprint, have voice recognition off, or Tayli cannot tell (a short or noisy line), **another person's words can be taken as your request and sent to Anthropic**, and Tayli may answer them. Lines it recognises as someone else's are shown as overheard and start nothing. **What Tayli says, others hear.** Tayli answers aloud, and a person whose voice it cannot verify can still talk to it. Its answer can include things from your current conversation or its memories about you (for example a city, a name or a plan mentioned earlier). The checks above stop an unverified voice from making Tayli act; they do not stop it from being told something Tayli already knows. Use hold to listen or quit Tayli when others should not hear. On Discord Tayli listens only to your own Discord audio, not to other callers; that audio includes anyone near your microphone, such as passengers in a car, and is not checked against your voiceprint. On the Mac, hold to listen (the default) keeps the microphone shut except while you hold the key; a Discord call has no hold to listen, so use Discord's own mute there.

Overheard lines stay on your Mac. They are sent to Anthropic (up to the last 2 minutes, at most 150 words, once) only if you turn on "Share recent room speech with Claude when I call Tayli", which is off by default and needs you to accept responsibility for telling people nearby, and then only when your request is about the room's conversation (for example "what did we just discuss?"), never for a plain command. Declining a voiceprint declines only storing the voiceprint, not transcription. Tell people nearby that Tayli is listening, and pause or quit it where people expect privacy.

Wake word and voice detection are not always reliable. Tayli may wake and start listening or answering when nobody said her name (similar-sounding words such as "Taylor" count as her name on purpose; a TV or other people can trigger her), and may miss her name, even with voice recognition (voiceprint) on. Whatever she hears while awake is transcribed as described above. Limitations: false wakes, missed wakes, mishearings and transcription errors, voice recognition mistaking people, echo (she may hear herself), and transcription of nearby speech while she listens. Tell other people in the room that Tayli listens and get their consent; if they do not consent, use hold to listen or quit Tayli. Liability is covered by section 11 of the Terms.

**Telling speakers apart.** With "Label other speakers" on (off by default), Tayli compares voices within one call, in memory only, to show "Person 1", "Person 2" in the chat. Nothing about their voice is stored or sent, no names are attached, and it is forgotten when the call ends. This may still count as biometric processing in some places (for example Illinois); turn it off there.

## 5. Voiceprints and biometric laws
A voiceprint is biometric data under several laws. Transcripts are not: they record what was said, not a model of the voice. Other people's voiceprints are **Not in this version**: today Tayli stores only your own, after your on-screen consent. The rules below are the ones that feature must meet before it ships; until then they describe a design, not a practice.
- **Hold, ask, then keep or forget.** Tayli needs some speech before it can tell a new voice apart, so during a conversation it holds a new voice only in memory, asks aloud ("Nice to meet you, is it OK if I remember your voice?"), and saves a voiceprint only when the yes matches that voice. A no, no answer, or a yes it cannot match to that voice means nothing is saved, and the held voice is forgotten when the conversation ends. Silence is never treated as consent.
- **Room context.** Tayli may save non-voice notes about a conversation, such as how many people were present, the place and time, so it understands your day. These are memories (section 2): visible, editable, deletable, and they contain no voice model. Your own voiceprint needs your acceptance of the in-app voiceprint step.
- **Purpose.** Only to recognise when it is you speaking: to keep other voices from being taken as yours, and as one check before Tayli acts on something spoken, including a spoken yes to a medium risk action at your Mac. High risk actions are never approved by voice. Approving by voice on Discord calls (an opt-in setting, Terms section 4) does not use your voiceprint; the approval record notes only whether a voice check ran and its result, never audio or the words. For every approval Tayli keeps a record on your Mac: the card's description, the tool, a fingerprint of its exact details, the risk level, the answer, how it was given (for example a tap on Discord) and when, with the app and terms version. It is a confidence score, not proof of identity. Never sold, leased, traded, shared or profited from; never sent off your Mac by Tayli.
- **Retention.** Deleted when the purpose ends, on request, when the size cap evicts it, or after 1 year without use, whichever is first. Your own voiceprint and its Keychain key are deleted when you delete it, and automatically once it has gone 1 year unused. This paragraph is the published retention and destruction schedule.
- **Security.** Encrypted on disk with a key held in your macOS Keychain.
- **Illinois (BIPA, 740 ILCS 14).** Requires informed written consent (a "written release") before collecting a biometric identifier, plus a public retention and destruction policy; private right of action with statutory damages. A spoken yes is very likely not a written release. Tayli stores no voiceprint but yours. Your own is created only after you accept the on-screen voiceprint notice, in addition to this policy; Tayli records when you accepted and refuses setup without it.
- **Texas (CUBI, Bus. & Com. Code 503.001).** Inform and get consent before capture for a commercial purpose; no sale; destroy within a reasonable time, no later than a year after the purpose ends; enforced by the Attorney General.
- **Washington (RCW 19.375 and My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373).** Notice and consent before enrolling a biometric identifier for a commercial purpose; MHMDA covers biometric data and is enforceable privately through the Consumer Protection Act.
- Also relevant: Colorado (biometric amendment to its privacy act), California (CCPA/CPRA sensitive personal information), New York City (biometric notice, for commercial establishments), EU/UK GDPR (biometric data for identification is special-category data).
Tayli follows the consent pattern anyway.
Tayli is for adults only. Never store a child's voiceprint; if a child says yes, delete it.

## 6. Recording calls and consent laws
Recording is off by default. When you turn it on, recordings are saved only on this Mac, and you can turn it off again in Settings. It is separate from voiceprints. Before you record, follow the law of every place a caller is in.
- **All-party consent** (everyone must agree), as commonly listed: California, Delaware, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan (disputed), Montana, Nevada (phone calls), New Hampshire, Oregon (in-person conversations), Pennsylvania, Washington; Connecticut (civil liability).
- **One-party consent**: US federal law and most other states. When callers are in different places, follow the strictest.
- Outside the US rules differ (e.g. EU/UK data protection, Canada). Discord's terms also apply.
A recording holds your microphone's audio, which includes anyone it picks up near you, Tayli's voice, and a text log of who said what. On Discord it holds only your audio and Tayli's: other callers are not recorded.

When recording is on, Tayli says a short notice when a call starts ("Heads up, I'm recording this call. You can turn that off in Settings."), and nothing is saved until the notice has played. On Discord it says the notice again when someone new joins. Announce recording is on by default; you can turn the announcement, or recording itself, off in Settings. You alone are responsible for telling people and getting the consent the law requires before you record, whether or not the announcement is on.

## 7. Children
Tayli is for adults (18+) only. The project collects nothing from anyone, so holds no children's data.

## 8. Your rights and choices
Because the Tayli project holds none of your data, you access, correct or delete it yourself, in the app or by deleting the data folder. For data held by Anthropic, a website, Discord, a safety service or an optional integration, contact them; we are not responsible for third parties or for telling you when their policies change. We do not sell or share personal information. We believe we are not a controller or business for data that stays on your Mac, but this is not settled.

## 9. Security
Tayli's safety measures: approval cards for risky actions, a safety reviewer for risky pages and actions, and limits on background tasks (site safety lists are **Not in this version**). Only your voiceprint is encrypted by Tayli (section 2); your Mac's security (password, FileVault, updates) adds protection. No system is perfectly secure.

## 10. Changes
We try our best to announce changes in the app, website and GitHub. You are responsible for checking. Changed versions apply once you accept them in the app.
